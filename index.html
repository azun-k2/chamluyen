Read aloud

Not reading well yet? They can still do it alone.

The younger grades have every question read aloud in a Northern or Southern accent, with a girl's or a boy's voice. Parents don't have to read each question, and children don't have to wait for anyone.

  • Northern or Southern accent, girl or boy voice
  • Tap “Nghe” to hear a question or each answer again
  • English questions are read in an American voice

Voices are available from lớp 1; the higher grades are being added.

Hints in steps

Wrong is fine. Let's look again together.

Children think first. When they are stuck the app helps a little at a time and never hands over the answer.

  1. 1
    A nudge on how to workShows how to get started.
  2. 2
    A bigger hintDoes the first part with them; they finish the rest.
  3. 3
    A step-by-step explanationThe buddy talks through it slowly, picture by picture.
Handwriting

Write letters on ruled lines, one stroke at a time.

The buddy shows where to put the pen down, which way to go and where to lift. Children write with a finger, and the app gently says so when a stroke starts in the wrong place.

  • All 29 letters, with a green start dot and arrows to follow
  • Watch how to write it again any time
  • Praise for finishing, never a comment on neatness
A buddy of their own

A friend the child creates.

Children pick an animal, fur colour, eyes, a hat and clothes. They can also draw a buddy of their own, or turn a photo into one. When they get something wrong the buddy encourages and never scolds.

  • Many animals, colours, hats, outfits and accessories
  • Draw a new buddy (the photo option is opened by a grown-up)
  • The buddy uses the child's name and reminds them to practise daily
Limited play time

Work first, play after, and keep to a schedule.

Earn enough stars and the child gets a few minutes of play. Each round lasts at most a minute, and when time is up the buddy sends them back to learning. Parents set every number.

  • Parents choose the stars needed, the minutes earned and the daily maximum
  • See how many minutes the child has played today
  • Redoing an old lesson earns no play time, so new work comes first
  • A nationwide board for friendly competition, plus a ranking by stars

FAQ

What parents ask

Which textbook series does it follow?

Kết nối tri thức với cuộc sống (“Connecting Knowledge with Life”): lessons follow the order and titles of that series, and the questions are our own. If your child uses another series (for example Chân trời sáng tạo), the subjects still follow the 2018 curriculum but lesson names and order may differ. Chăm luyện is an independent app, not a publisher product.

Does it read the questions aloud?

Yes, from lớp 1 (the higher grades are being added), in a Northern or Southern accent. A grade without a voice yet shows the text instead.

Does it teach new lessons?

No. Chăm luyện is where children practise and take short tests for each grade's subjects. It sits beside them while they work, like a patient older friend.

Will a hint give the answer away?

No. Hints go in steps: how to work, then a bigger hint, then an explanation. The app never gives the answer straight away.

Can a child play all day?

No. They must earn stars to get play time, and parents set the daily maximum.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later), Mac (macOS 14 or later) and Android (8.0 or later), with one account for all. See Get the app for which stores are live.

How do I get a full account?

Every subject has a few free sample lessons. For full access, contact us through the Support page.

Is my child's data safe?

The app has no ads or tracking. Without an account, nothing about the child leaves the device. Details are in the privacy policy.

The short version

Chăm luyện has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. A grown-up can buy a plan in the app with their Apple account. Without an account, nothing about your child leaves the device except downloads of voice files. Signing in to an account, which a grown-up creates in the app or we create on request, adds the few things listed below and nothing more.

What goes over the internet

Voice files. One voice comes with the app. The others are downloaded from the app's own file server (packs.khangazun.com), when your child picks one, a couple of minutes after the app opens, when Settings is opened, and when the app goes to the background. Each download asks for a sound file by its name; it carries no information about your child, their progress or their name.

What the file server sees. Like any website, the server, run by Cloudflare, receives your device's IP address and the usual details of a web request when a file is downloaded. Cloudflare handles that under its own privacy policy. We receive nothing from these downloads.

Checked before use. Every downloaded voice is checked against a fingerprint built into the app, and is discarded if it does not match.

Your account, only when you sign in. A grown-up can create the account in the app: it asks for a phone number, we send a six-digit code to that number by SMS, and the grown-up chooses a password (we can also create an account on request). Our server (Cloudflare Workers and a Cloudflare D1 database) then holds the phone number, the parent's name if given, a salted hash of the password (never the password itself), the plan records (package, start and end dates), the devices that are signed in (when each one signed in and was last used; the app does not send the model or the system of the device) and the date of the last sign-in. It is used only to sign in, to know whether a plan covers today, and to help you when you write to us. It is not sold, not shared for advertising, and not used for analytics or tracking. To stop automated abuse of the sign-up code, the server also counts how many sign-up requests come from one internet address in a day: it keeps only a one-way hash of the address (never the address itself) with the time, and deletes it after 24 hours.

Plans bought in the app. On iPhone, iPad and Mac a grown-up can buy a plan (1 month, 6 months or 1 year) with their Apple account; Apple takes the payment and we never see any card or payment details. The app sends Apple's signed receipt for the purchase to our server, which checks Apple's signature and then adds the period to the account; we keep the transaction number with the plan record. A plan can be restored with Restore Purchases in the app while the account still exists; deleting the account removes the plan records, and a purchase cannot then be moved to another account.

The national board, only with an account that has a plan. The app sends your child's best score in each break game, and the total number of stars collected in each grade, together with the name typed in the app. Other children see that name, the scores and stars and the last four digits of the account's phone number, so two children with the same name can be told apart. Names are checked on our server, and an unsuitable one is shown as "Bạn nhỏ". Nothing else about your child's progress is sent.

Study reminders, only if a grown-up turns them on. After a few lessons the app can offer a daily reminder, behind a question for a grown-up. A reminder is a notification made and shown by the device itself at the chosen time; nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and the grown-up can turn it off in the app or in the device's settings.

The buddy picture on the board. By default the board shows only a built-in buddy look, which carries no personal information. If a grown-up chooses to give the buddy a picture the child drew or a photo, a small copy of it (about 96 pixels) is sent to our server and shown next to the child's name on the national board, so other children who open the board can see it. The picture is checked automatically by an AI image-checking service on Cloudflare before it is shown, and our staff can block it; until it passes, or if it is blocked, other children see a built-in buddy or a red cross instead. Please do not use a photo of a face you would not want other children to see. Changing the buddy picture replaces the copy, and deleting the account removes it.

Game ideas you send. A grown-up with an account that has a plan can send us an idea for a new game from the game picker: a short text (up to 300 characters) and, if they choose, one picture, which the app shrinks before sending. The text and the picture are sent to our server and read by our staff, together with the account's phone number so we can tell which family sent it. Please do not put names, addresses or photos of people in an idea. They are used only to decide which games to make, are not shown to anyone else, and are deleted when the account is deleted.

Question reports. A grown-up with an account that has a plan can tell us that a question looks wrong, with the small flag at the top of a lesson (after a short question meant for adults). The report holds the question's code, the question text and answer choices as the child saw them, the answer the app counts as right, the answer the child had picked, the reason chosen (for example a wrong answer or a typing mistake), an optional note of up to 300 characters, the app version and the platform. It is sent to our server and read by our staff, together with the account's phone number so we can tell which family sent it. Please do not put names or addresses in the note. Reports are used only to correct questions, are not shown to anyone else, and are deleted when the account is deleted.

What is stored, and where

Your child's profile, on the device: the name typed in, the buddy, stars, progress and settings, kept in the app's own storage. A small copy of the progress is also kept in the device's Keychain (iPhone, iPad and Mac), so that installing the app again does not lose it; it stays on the device and is not sent to us. If your device backs itself up, for example to iCloud, the backup can include the profile, as it does other apps' data.

Downloaded voices are kept in a separate folder that is left out of backups, since they can be downloaded again.

Deleting the app removes the files; the small Keychain copy stays on the device for a reinstall.

Deleting an account. A signed-in grown-up can delete the account in Settings with the password. That removes the phone number, name, plan records, signed-in devices and board scores from our server. Our back-office activity log may keep a short record of staff actions (who, what, when) that refers to the account.

Feedback email

If a grown-up taps Góp ý cho người làm ứng dụng in Settings, the app opens a new email in your mail app with a short template: the app version, the device model and system version, the chosen accent, whether the voice is on, and the number of stars. Nothing is sent until you press Send, and you can change or delete any of it first.

Children

Chăm luyện is made for children. It asks only for a first name, so the buddy can greet the child; that name stays on the device unless a signed-in account is on the national board, in which case the name, best scores, stars and, if chosen, the buddy picture are shown to other children, as described above. Children do not create accounts or buy anything: accounts and plans are for a grown-up. Everything the child hears was written and recorded in advance; no AI talks to your child.
